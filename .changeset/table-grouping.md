Table grouping: `groupBy` on `SpeelTable` and `SpeelEntityTable` groups the matched rows into nested, collapsible groups, outermost level first — a field name, a column ref (`p.Status`), or a descriptor with its own `by` (a computed value), `label`, `header`, `collapsed` (the starting state) and `order`. Each group header shows its row count (`Status: Open (12)` by default); a toggle in the header cell in front of the columns expands or collapses every group (as does a click on a group header's text, for that group), and the handles gain `expandAllGroups()` / `collapseAllGroups()`. Collapse state is session-only. Grouping takes precedence over paging: a grouped table is unpaged (with a one-time console warning when a page size is set) and pages where it left off once grouping is removed. Export, print and `onMatchedRowsChange` follow the grouped row order. Skins render groups from the new `TableProps.groups` (`TableGroupNode`) and `onToggleGroup`; both built-in skins do. Default group headers are muted progressively by level (up to three steps). Both skins offset data rows under their innermost group and draw that expand/collapse-all toggle in the leading header cell, driven by the new `TableProps.allGroupsCollapsed` / `onToggleAllGroups`. `SpeelEntityTableHandle` now also carries `exportXlsx()`, and the shadcn skin's `Button` now renders its `iconName`. `GroupDescriptor`, `GroupSpec`, `RowGroup` and `TableGroupNode` are exported.
