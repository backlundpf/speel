Table grouping: `groupBy` on `SpeelTable` and `SpeelEntityTable` groups the matched rows into nested, collapsible groups, outermost level first — a field name, a column ref (`p.Status`), or a descriptor with its own `by` (a computed value), `label`, `header`, `collapsed` (the starting state) and `order`. Each group header shows its row count (`Status: Open (12)` by default); a toolbar button expands or collapses every group, and the handles gain `expandAllGroups()` / `collapseAllGroups()`. Collapse state is session-only. Grouping takes precedence over paging: a grouped table is unpaged (with a one-time console warning when a page size is set) and pages where it left off once grouping is removed. Export, print and `onMatchedRowsChange` follow the grouped row order. Skins render groups from the new `TableProps.groups` (`TableGroupNode`) and `onToggleGroup`; both built-in skins do. `SpeelEntityTableHandle` now also carries `exportXlsx()`. `GroupDescriptor`, `GroupSpec`, `RowGroup` and `TableGroupNode` are exported.
