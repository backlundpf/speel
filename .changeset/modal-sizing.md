Surfaces: modals and panels take `style`, CSS lengths for `width` and `maxWidth` (both) and `height`, `maxHeight`, `top` and `left` (modal only) (#101). `width`/`height` replace what `size` sets, caps yield to the screen, and `height: "auto"` fits a modal to its content, growing and shrinking with it (centred, or downward when `top` is set) until the cap, after which the body scrolls. A user drag or resize takes over (auto height resumes when the modal is next opened). With no `style`, every surface looks as before. `useSurfaces().confirm({ title, message, confirmText, cancelText, destructive })` resolves `true` or `false` from a regular content-sized `SpeelModal` with two actions; a destructive one opens on Cancel, any other on the confirm button (#102). `SpeelAction.autoFocus` gives an action's button focus when its modal opens (found by id: `ButtonProps` gains an optional `id`, and `SpeelActionBar` an `idPrefix`), and `SpeelModal` takes `ariaDescribedBy`. `DialogProps` gains `style`, `ariaDescribedBy` and `initialFocus`, and `DrawerProps` gains `style`; a hand-written `SurfaceApi` now needs `confirm`. Skins: `useModalFrame` (plus `modalFrameCss`, `panelMaxWidth`, `cssLengthPx` and the `DIALOG_*` presets) supersedes `useDragResize`, which is deprecated but unchanged; `useResizable` takes `measure`. The shadcn dialog now honours `defaultFullscreen`.
